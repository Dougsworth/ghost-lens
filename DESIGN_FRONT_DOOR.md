# GHOST front door and learning UI (design spec)

Goal: anyone feels invited to learn anything, and gets a small win in the first 30 seconds.
Principles: Snap Spectacles design guidelines, Impeccable (visual), Intent (UX). No dark patterns.

## Visual language
- Materials: washi paper and sumi ink. One accent colour only: vermilion, used ONLY for the stamp and success moments.
- Type: one clean sans for UI plus the kana. Three sizes only: Title (big, bold), Body, Meta. Large enough to read at arm's length instantly.
- Shapes: one corner radius everywhere. No nested cards, no icon tiles, no pill chips, no thin border plus soft shadow, no glows, no gradients, no pulsing dots, no "AI is thinking" spinner.
- Text always sits on paper or a soft backdrop for legibility on the additive display.

## Front door (first thing you see)
- A single sheet of paper floating centred, slightly low, about 1 m away.
- Title in brush style ink: "What do you want to learn?"
- One primary action: a large ink circle with a mic glyph. Pinch it or just say the skill.
- Under it, three handwritten suggestion lines that change each launch, e.g. "write my name in Japanese", "an Em chord on guitar", "sign thank you". Pinch one to start.
- Below, small and quiet: "Your notebook" showing skills you've started, each with a few ink stamps. This is progress, not pressure: no streak guilt, no timers.

## While the AI builds the lesson
- No spinner. The ghost brush sketches the lesson outline onto the paper as it arrives: the skill name, then 3 to 6 short step titles appear one by one in ink.
- If it takes long, one honest line: "Writing your lesson..." Never fake progress.

## The lesson
- One step at a time. Top: big single word state (WATCH, YOUR TURN, NICE). Middle: the thing to learn. Bottom: one line tip.
- First step is always tiny and easy so the first stamp lands within 30 seconds.
- AI coach tip after each attempt: one specific friendly sentence, spoken and written. Encouraging, never shaming.
- Never a dead end: after 2 misses, "Keep going" is offered.

## Finish
- Big vermilion stamp, page turns to a summary: what you learned, one line of praise from the coach, and two choices: "Practise again" or "Learn something new".
- The skill is added to "Your notebook" with its stamps.

## Motion
- Purposeful only: ink drawing in, page turns, the stamp. Short (0.2 to 0.4 s), no bouncy overshoot, no decorative staggering.
